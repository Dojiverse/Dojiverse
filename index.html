Automated Trading System

TypeScript · Python · PostgreSQL · Supabase (Edge Functions, pg_cron) · AWS EC2 · Polygon.io · Tradier & DAS Trader APIs · SEC EDGAR
Internal quantitative trading product · live capital

A fully automated long/short trading system built on statistical edge from 3 years of classified market data. Fifteen serverless functions orchestrate data ingestion, signal detection, and execution across two broker APIs, with position sizing validated through 10,000-iteration Monte Carlo simulation. It trades autonomously from pre-market to close.

About DOJi

DOJi LLC is a Virginia-based development studio founded and led by Pedro Petcov (B.S. Computer Science, NYU Shanghai). Every engagement gets founder-level ownership: the person who designs your architecture also writes the code, wires the payments, hardens the security, and stays for operations after launch.

The studio has shipped three production products, run a 3-person engineering internship program with structured code review, and builds with AI-accelerated development workflows to deliver at a pace small teams normally can't.
